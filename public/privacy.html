Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11 (Beta). Please read carefully before using Pulse.

Pulse is available only to persons aged 18 or above who are legally competent to enter into a contract. By creating an account or using the service you accept these Terms and this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

2. Beta service

Pulse is a beta (testing) service. Features may change, be interrupted or be withdrawn without notice, and data may be reset. The service is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis without warranties of any kind, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

3. Information we process

Account data (email address, display name, age, city, occupation, profile text, optional photo and role-specific details such as job or matrimonial attributes); content you create (posts, messages, images, CVs you choose to upload); records of consent; and, only if you enable Nearby, approximate location rounded to about 1 km. Your email address and phone number are not displayed to other members. A phone number, if you add one, is released only to a member you approve, subject to the Premium conditions in clause 6.

4. Purposes and sharing

We process data to operate the service, match and connect members, prevent abuse and comply with law. Profiles are visible to verified members according to the mode you choose. A CV is shared only with the recruiter of a job you apply to. AI features (Saathi, AI Mediator, Gaana Studio) transmit the text you submit to Google Gemini to generate replies; the Mediator reads a conversation only after both participants agree. Infrastructure is provided by Google Firebase. We do not sell personal data. Voice and video calls connect directly between the two members' devices (peer-to-peer) and are encrypted end-to-end using the WebRTC standard (DTLS-SRTP); call audio and video never pass through or are stored on Pulse's servers, and Pulse has no feature to record a call without the visible on-screen notice and consent of both participants. No app, including Pulse, can prevent another participant from recording or photographing their own screen during a call; do not share anything in a call you would not want recorded.

5. Conduct and enforcement

You must not harass, threaten, defraud, impersonate, post unlawful, obscene, hateful or infringing content, solicit money, OTPs or banking details, or misuse another member data. We may remove content, restrict features or suspend accounts, including automatically flagged accounts after review. Members may block and report any account.

6. Contact details and Premium

Requesting another member contact number requires Premium status and the explicit approval of that member, who may revoke access at any time. Premium is activated manually by the Pulse team once payment is confirmed. Any attempt to obtain contact details by other means is prohibited.

7. Recruitment and matrimonial notices

Pulse does not verify employers, candidates, or matrimonial claims and is not a party to any employment, engagement or marriage arrangement. Never pay a fee to obtain a job or interview. Verify identity, credentials and family details independently and meet only in public places.

8. Your rights

You may edit your profile, pause visibility, turn off location, withdraw applications, revoke contact approvals and delete your account from the Me tab, which removes your profile, posts, CV and AI history. Messages sent in shared conversations may remain visible to the other participant. Requests and complaints may be made through the in-app Report function.

9. Limitation of liability and governing law

To the extent permitted by law, Pulse and its owner are not liable for member conduct, meetings between members, or indirect or consequential loss. These Terms are governed by the laws of India and subject to the courts of competent jurisdiction in India.

9. Private Talk (anonymous support talks)

Private Talk lets two adults talk anonymously. The other person sees only a random alias, never your name, photo, profile, phone number or email. Text, voice notes and calls are exchanged between the two members; chat and voice notes are erased when the talk ends. For video, a mask is applied to your face on your own device before anything is sent, and the picture is hidden if your face cannot be found. You may disguise your voice. Calls are relayed through a TURN relay so the other member cannot see your IP address (you may switch this off). Pulse keeps a technical identifier of each member in a talk request so that abuse can be stopped and reports reviewed. If you report a member, the last messages of that talk are sent to Pulse for review. Anonymity is not absolute: you can still be recognised if you give your name, show your surroundings or the other person knows your voice. Pulse is peer support, not medical or legal advice. In a crisis call Tele-MANAS 14416 or emergency 112.

10. Device registration and owner controls

When you first open the app you are asked to agree to device registration. We record a random device ID, your account ID, app version, language, time zone, device type (web or Android) and the time of last use. We do not record contacts, messages or precise location in this record. Purpose: security, abuse and fraud prevention, and support. In case of abuse the Pulse owner may lock Pulse on a device, erase Pulse data stored on that device and sign it out, or ask Android to show its uninstall dialog (which you must confirm). These actions run while the app is open and online. The device record is deleted when you delete your account.

11. Your data: access, export and deletion

You can download a copy of your data in the app (Me → Download my data) and delete your account and its data in the app (Me → Delete account). You can also request deletion at the address on the Delete account page of this site. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, Saathi history, presence and device record. Reports about other members and records we must keep to prevent abuse or comply with law may be retained for a limited time.

12. Permissions used on Android

Camera and microphone: calls, video and voice notes. Location (optional): the Nearby feature, rounded to about 1 km. Notifications: calls and messages while the app is open. Bluetooth: choosing a headset for calls. Foreground service: keeping a call running while the app is in the background. Package deletion request: lets the owner ask Android to show its own uninstall dialog for Pulse. Pulse does not read your contacts, SMS or files.

13. Children

Pulse is for adults aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly allow minors. If you believe a minor is using Pulse, report the account in the app and it will be removed.
