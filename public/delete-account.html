Pulse · Terms & Privacy · Delete account

Delete your Pulse account and data

In the app (fastest)

Open Pulse → Me → Delete account and confirm. This removes your profile, posts, Saathi history, presence and the record of the device you used. If the app asks you to log in again first, do so and repeat.

Download a copy first

Me → Download my data saves a file with your profile, posts and Saathi history.

If you cannot open the app

Sign in on this website (the same address as the app) and use the same steps. If you can no longer sign in, send a deletion request to the support address shown on Pulse’s Google Play page, from the email address of your account, and we will delete the account within 30 days.

What is kept

Reports about other members and records we must keep to prevent abuse or comply with law may be kept for a limited time. Anonymous Private Talk chats and voice notes are already erased when the talk ends.
